# Competitive landscape, November 2026

`defiformal-competition-tech-strategy.html` is the IOG Technical Intelligence report 'DeFi·Formal: Competitive Landscape and Technical Strategy' by Andreas Kendziorra and Stefan Contiu, data as of 2026-11-06. It is a single self-contained HTML file; open it in a browser. A hosted copy is at https://input-output-hk.github.io/techint/defiformal-competition-tech-strategy.html.

What it contains:

- Section 1: capability grading of 13 tools and DeFiFormal (graded from this repository at `2b60322`) on DeFi primitives and properties.
- Section 2: benchmark measurements on two shared workloads, Suite 1.0 and rational-1, with DeFiFormal measured under the same contract.
- Section 3: maturity and activity of the 13 tools. DeFiFormal was not yet assessed under the rubric.
- Section 4: five gaps to close and the 24-task list that `openspec/changes/competitive-landscape-adoption/` turns into a change proposal.

Measurements are of abstract benchmark models, not proofs about deployed code, and do not rank tools. The raw measurement records are held in IOG's private assessment repository; `evidence-index.json` in the change folder names them.
